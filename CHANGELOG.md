# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Opt-in anonymous sharing.** After 20 rated prompts, rightsize asks once whether to share anonymous numbers with its author. On a yes, at most once an hour it sends what the run log gained since: task types, models and levels, token guesses, conversation size, estimated and real costs, outcomes (rework, step-ups, a count of mid-turn raises), Sonnet checks and 👍/👎, with times rounded to the hour, a random install ID and a one-way code linking each 👍/👎 to its prompt. Prompts, replies, code, file names, paths, tool and server names, raise reasons and session IDs are never sent. **Share anonymous feedback** in `/config` (off by default) turns it on or off; turning it on again starts a new install ID. Nothing is sent while `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_NONESSENTIAL_TRAFFIC` is set. The README has a Privacy section.
- **Haiku's rating is scored against what happens.** rightsize counts, per task type and model, when the rating turned out wrong: a hand-off that needed rework (more than one subagent call), a lowered turn that stepped back up or was raised mid-turn, an unchanged turn raised mid-turn, a 👎, or a Sonnet check that disagreed. The matching hits are a hand-off without rework, a lowered turn that held, a 👍 and an agreeing check. A type needs 5 outcomes on a model before its own rate counts, else the model's rate across types does (also from 5). Only the last 30 days of the run log count, and nothing leaves your machine. At a 30% rate, hand-downs of that kind of work to that model stop, and so does lowering effort on it, until the rate falls back under 30%.
- **Starting-point estimates weigh that rate.** Where Haiku's rating of a start's model has been wrong for a task type, that share of its prompts is replayed as if the model had been rated unable, so the cost and the count of prompts sent to a stronger model rise with it.
- **Sonnet spot-checks Haiku.** On every 50th rated prompt (the first check on the 50th), rightsize sends the same rating prompt to Sonnet at low effort in the background; the prompt is never delayed. The answer is logged as a `check` line carrying Haiku's `fit`, Sonnet's `fit` and the check's cost, which the report counts as overhead. A check that fails or can't be read adds only its cost. A check's cost that lands in the ledger while a turn runs is left out of that turn's cost. **Rater check** in `/config` (on by default) turns it off.
- **👍 and 👎 in the band.** A hand-off's result and a lowering now show 👍 and 👎. Pressing one writes a `feedback` line to `rightsize-runs.jsonl`, takes the buttons off the band so it can't be pressed twice, and shows a short thanks. Nothing is sent anywhere. Each run line now carries an `id`, which the feedback line names as `ref`.
- `/rightsize report` says how often Haiku's judgement was wrong (and the hand-downs and lowerings paused for it), and `/rightsize` shows the Rater check setting. Check and feedback lines are not rated runs.
- **Start suggestions.** Once a week at session start, rightsize estimates what the last 30 days of rated prompts would have cost from Sonnet and Opus at medium and high, replaying each prompt through its own planner with your rules, thresholds and calibration. The band suggests switching with `/model` when another start is at least 15% cheaper (25% while it has under 20 measured main-chat runs) and sends no more than 1 prompt in 10 to a stronger model, or when over 15% of prompts on the current start needed a stronger model or a mid-turn raise. It waits for 50 rated prompts in 30 days and 20 on the current start. **Not now** quiets the same suggestion for 14 days; **Stop suggesting**, or **Start suggestions** in `/config`, turns it off. `/rightsize report` always shows the comparison. rightsize never changes the model itself.
- Run log lines carry `normal` (the session's own level, before rightsize's change) and `context` (the conversation's size when the prompt was rated), which the starting-point check replays from. Older lines use the run's level and the median size.
- The README recommends starting on Sonnet 5.5 or Opus 5.5 at medium.
- **Subscription wording leads with usage.** On a subscription, `/rightsize report` puts the share of the 5-hour window kept first, then the share of usage saved, with dollars marked API-equivalent; a line at the top says dollar amounts are API-equivalent. Before runs show the window rate, the share of usage leads. A hand-off's result says "API-equivalent" instead of "At API prices".
- **Idle warning on a subscription.** The idle question now comes up on a subscription too. It gives the cost as a share of the 5-hour window (from the last 30 days of runs) with API-equivalent dollars beside it, or API-equivalent dollars alone until runs show the rate. The **Idle warning** bar is compared in API-equivalent dollars.

## 0.6.0 (2026-10-24)

- **Estimates follow the work each model does at each effort.** The same task takes different output tokens and tool calls depending on the model and its level: Opus at low effort takes far fewer steps than at medium, Haiku writes more than Sonnet, Fable more than Opus. A table of work per model and level (Sonnet as the baseline, so its estimates do not change) now scales the rated output tokens and steps for the main chat and for subagents. The values come from Anthropic's cost and intelligence guide and Artificial Analysis, with guesses interpolated; calibration corrects them.
- **Close calls are broken by a task-type guide that fades as runs come in.** When two models that could take the work land within 25% of each other in estimated cost, rightsize no longer lets noise pick the cheaper one: it takes the model a task-type table names (Haiku for lookups, searches, bulk edits, extraction and summaries; Sonnet for writing, review, features, debugging, refactors and the rest; Opus for design), or the stronger model when the table's pick is not among them. The 25% shrinks to nothing as the two routes' runs are measured (zero at 20 runs, counted on the one with fewer). It only chooses among routes that already pass the hand-off share or fit the work; it never makes a hand-off or overrides a "would struggle". One close call in five takes the other route instead, counted in the store (`ties`), so both get measured. The same rule picks the model for Claude's own subagents. Run log lines carry `tie`, and `/rightsize report` says how many close calls the guide decided and how many were explored.
- **Calibration starts over once.** It is stored under a new key (`calibration-v2`) because the old ratios were learned against the old estimates and would count the change twice. The old key is ignored, not read or deleted.

## 0.5.0 (2026-10-23)

- **Claude's moved subagents are measured and learned from.** Each one's own requests are priced, and when it answers (often after the turn that started it, for a background one) rightsize logs a `subagent` line with its real cost and its raw estimates on its model and on the parent's, and teaches `<type>:subagent`, `:<model>` and `:<model>:<level>` calibration from it. The next sizing uses those numbers. A stopped subagent is logged and teaches nothing; a resumed one counts again from zero.
- `/rightsize report` counts these subagents ("N subagents Claude started ran on a cheaper model") and adds their saving: the real cost scaled by the parent's estimate over the chosen model's.
- A subagent's first request no longer slips through at Claude's effort when the engine starts it before the spawn settles: a step from a subagent whose id is not known yet is matched to the pending spawn by its description and type in the agent list. This covers the hand-off's subagent and moved ones.
- The rating no longer asks for `size`; routing has not used it since model and effort were rated together. Older replies that still carry it read as before.
- Raise when stuck: a Bash command run again with a new `description` counts as the same call, so "the same tool call failed twice" fires. Found in a live session, where three failing `false` calls only tripped "3 tool calls failed".
- The fixed-context advice in `/rightsize report` names the fixes for its biggest parts (skills and plugins, MCP servers, CLAUDE.md, agents) instead of always leading with MCP.
- The idle question says "1 minute", not "1 minutes".
- **Idle warning.** On API billing, when a session under 200K tokens sat idle past the cache's life, rightsize asks before the next prompt (short ones too) writes the whole conversation to the cache again, with the cost of sending, of /compact, and that /clear is free. Hold it and the prompt goes back in the box. Each session's last turn is kept in the store, so a resume days later is caught too. Asked once per idle gap. **Idle warning** in `/config` sets the bar (over $0.10 by default) or turns it off. Subscriptions are left to Claude Code's own warning, and sessions over 200K to its idle compaction.
- **Fixed context in the report.** Each run line logs its main-chat requests and the tokens every request carried that rightsize can't trim: loaded MCP tools (by server), CLAUDE.md and memory, agent descriptions, skills and commands, with what re-reading them from the cache cost at least. `/rightsize report` adds them up for 30 days, names the biggest MCP servers, and says how to cut it.
- **Raise when stuck.** When a turn shows Claude is stuck, effort goes up one level for the rest of that turn. The signs: the same tool call failing twice, 3 tool calls failing, the same call running 3 times, or a turn passing 3x the rated steps (at least 15). Each sign counts once a turn; at most two raises a turn, never to max. It follows the Raising rule: automatic raises without asking, "Ask" (or no rule yet) asks mid-turn, never does nothing. A refused call is not a sign; a hand-off's main chat is not raised; nothing is raised when a usage window is over 90% used. Raised turns teach calibration nothing and claim no saving; run log lines carry `escalations`, and `/rightsize report` lists them under what went wrong. **Raise when stuck** in `/config` turns it off.
- `tsc` passes on the tests again: the fake engine's rate limits carry `resetsAt`.
- **Budget pacing.** On a subscription, rightsize moves its thresholds by how fast the 5-hour and 7-day windows are being used (share used over share of time gone), using whichever window would stop you first. On pace to run out early (over 1.2): hand off under 85% of staying and lower under 97%. Much of a window left near its reset (under 0.6): hand off only under 50%, lower only under 80%, and raise effort without asking when the rule is "Ask". Over 90% used: hand off under 95%, lower whenever it fits, and skip raises and stronger models. The first 15% of a window is not paced. The band says when pacing changed a decision, and run log lines carry `pace`. **Budget pacing** in `/config` turns it off. Choices set to never or stay are kept.
- Run log lines carry the rater's guesses behind each estimate (`read`, `write`, `steps`), so a bad guess can be told from a bad cost model.
- **Claude's own subagents.** When Claude starts a general-purpose subagent without naming a model, it would run on Claude's own model. rightsize now rates the subagent's brief with Haiku and runs it on the cheapest weaker model that fits, at that model's level, when that costs under the hand-off threshold. It never picks a stronger model, and it leaves alone subagents that name a model, other agent types (they may set their own), forks, workflow agents, resumes, teammates and subagents started by subagents. The band says so (`Ran Claude's subagent on Haiku (low) instead of Opus.`), and run log lines carry `resized`. **Claude's subagents** in `/config` turns it off.
- A hand-off now counts as taken when Claude's Agent call names the full model id (`claude-haiku-5-5`), not only the alias.
- **Workflow guard (ultracode).** Once Claude runs a workflow in a session, rightsize stops handing work off for the rest of that session, and turns from then on teach calibration nothing and claim no saving: a workflow runs on in the background, so its agents' cost lands in later turns too. Prompts are still rated and effort still changes. `/rightsize report` lists these turns on their own line; run log lines carry `workflows` and `afterWorkflow`.
- Main-chat calibration is kept per task type, per model and per model at each level, like hand-offs: expensive runs at high no longer move the estimate for low.
- **Model and effort together.** The rating gives each model's own level (or says it would struggle), and the cost model prices every model at its level. On Opus at medium, work Opus needs high for can go to Haiku at high instead of raising Opus. Of the cheaper models that fit, the cheapest route wins; the comparison counts the switch a raise would cost. Hand-downs no longer need the work rated "bulky": the cost decides. The older one-level rating still reads.
- The hand-off's subagent runs at the level its model needs: rightsize sets its effort on each of its requests, instead of relying on the note. Other subagents keep their own level.
- Hand-off calibration is kept per task type, per model and per model at each level; each finer key starts from the one above it.
- `rightsize-runs.jsonl` lines carry each model's rated level (`fit`) and a hand-off's real subagent level (`subLevel`).
- **Hand-off threshold** (default 70%) and **Lowering threshold** (default 90%) in `/config`: how much cheaper a subagent or a lower effort level must be before rightsize uses it. `/rightsize` shows the values in use.
- Calibration from sessions running at the same time is merged, not overwritten: each save adds its new runs to the stored record.
- A lowered turn that stepped back up no longer counts toward calibration, since part of it ran at the normal level.
- A band above the prompt (CLI and desktop app) shows what rightsize did, with buttons to undo it, clear a session switch, change the rule for that case, or close it. It also shows a hand-off's result, which no longer takes a transcript line. **Band** in `/config` (or ⋯ on the band) sets when it shows: when it acts (default), always, or off.
- Billing is read off each response's rate limits: five-hour and seven-day windows mean a subscription, a spend limit means a gateway. rightsize asks once only when they show nothing, and keeps the answer across sessions.
- Each turn's actual cost comes from the session's cost ledger (the `/cost` total), leaving out the Haiku rating call. Where there is no ledger, it prices the token counts from `prices.json`. Cache writes are priced at the 1-hour TTL by default.
- Every rated turn is logged to `rightsize-runs.jsonl` with its session, the change made and suggested, the rating call's cost, the rate-limit windows, its duration and its subagent calls.
- `/rightsize report`: prompts rated and changed, the saving net of rating calls, the share of the usage window it kept for subscribers, and what went wrong, for this session, 7 days and 30 days.
- The monthly price check is claimed before the page is fetched, so two sessions starting at once check only once. A failed check gives the claim back and stays due.

## 0.4.0 (2026-10-23)

First public release, renamed from effort-advisor.

- Q1–Q4: lowering, raising, a stronger model and a cheaper model, each asked once and remembered on request.
- A cost model that counts cache reads and writes, output and thinking, the cache rewrite an effort switch may cause, and the brief, report, check and rework of a subagent.
- Calibration from the API's real token usage, per task type and route.
- Subagent hand-off through a hidden note beside the prompt, with a "Done means" check and at most 2 reworks.
- Monthly price check against Anthropic's pricing page; "check now" in `/config` and `/rightsize prices`.
- One log line per measured effort switch (`~/.claude/mods-logs/rightsize-switches.jsonl`), to learn whether Claude Code keeps the cache when effort changes.
